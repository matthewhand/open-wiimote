TILT STEERING

        

          
          
Hold the remote horizontally like a NES controller: rolling it steers, pitching it looks up and down. The axis selector picks the roll, the pitch or the total tilt for the single axis modes.

          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
          
Invert steering and invert pitch flip the combined mode, invert tilt axis flips the single axis modes. Original Wii Remotes support tilt without an accessory. MotionPlus gyro mapping is planned for a future release.
