# Example capture for
#   dotnet run --project src/OpenWiimote.App -- --replay tools/sample-reports.txt
#
# One report per line, hex bytes separated by spaces. Lines starting with # and blank
# lines are ignored. Byte values come from the report layouts documented in
# WiimoteReportParser (offsets follow the Linux hid-wiimote driver).
#
# 0x80 is the zero point of an accelerometer axis and 0x9A is one gravity of it, so
# 80 80 9A is a remote lying flat with Z carrying the gravity.

# Status report: LED 1 lit, button One held, 100% battery, no extension
20 00 02 18 00 00 D7

# Core buttons only, nothing pressed
30 00 00

# Core buttons and accelerometer: A and B held, remote lying flat
31 00 0C 80 80 9A

# Core buttons and accelerometer: rolled to the right by about 33 degrees
31 00 00 8E 80 96

# A truncated report: the id is decoded and nothing else
31 00

# Reply to the read of the extension identifier at 0xA400FA: a Nunchuk
21 00 00 50 00 FA 00 00 A4 20 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00

# Buttons, accelerometer and extension: a Nunchuk with its stick centred, nothing held
35 00 00 80 80 9A 80 80 80 80 80 03 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00 00

# Accepting a write of the extension encryption register
22 00 00 16 00
